## A1. Getting Started

### Creating an Account
1. Go to **sokoyetu.co.ke** or open the SokoYetu app
2. Click **Register** (top right)
3. Fill in your full name, email or Kenyan phone (07XX / 01XX), and a password (min 8 chars)
4. Click **Create Account**
5. Verify your email — enter the 6-digit code sent to your inbox
6. Done — your account is ready

**Tip:** Sign in instantly with your **Google account** — no password needed.

### Signing In
Click **Sign In**, enter your email/phone and password, then click **Login**.

**Forgot password?** Click Forgot Password, enter your email or phone, then follow the reset link sent by email or SMS.

## A2. Browsing & Searching

### Homepage Sections
| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Flash Deals** | Time-limited discounts with a countdown timer |
| **Categories** | Browse by product type |
| **Featured Products** | Hand-picked top listings |
| **Top Vendors** | Highest-rated shops |
| **New Arrivals** | Products listed in the last 7 days |

### Searching for a Product
Click the search bar at the top, type your query (e.g. "rice 50kg", "Samsung phone"), then press Enter or tap 🔍.

### Using Filters
After searching, click **⚙️ Filters** to narrow results:

| Filter | How to use |
|---|---|
| **Min / Max Price (KES)** | Set your budget range |
| **Location** | Pick a county |
| **Rating** | Show 4-star vendors and above |
| **Sort** | Price, rating, or newest first |

Click **Apply**. Active filters appear as tags — click ✕ on a tag to remove it individually, or **Reset** to clear all.

### Product Page
Each product shows: photos (tap to zoom), price, vendor name and rating, stock level, customer reviews with photos, and bulk pricing if available.

## A3. Placing an Order

### Adding to Cart
On any product, click **Add to Cart**. The 🛒 icon at the top updates with your count.

In the cart, tap **+** / **−** to change quantities, or 🗑️ to remove an item.

### Checkout Steps

**Step 1 — Delivery Address**
Select a saved address or click **Add New Address** (name, phone, county, town, street). Tick **Save this address** for future use.

**Step 2 — Delivery Partner**
| Partner | Speed | From |
|---|---|---|
| Boda Network | 45–90 min (Nairobi) | KES 150 |
| Sendy | 2–4 hrs (Nairobi) | KES 200 |
| G4S Kenya | 1–3 days (Nationwide) | KES 350 |
| DHL Kenya | 1–5 days (Nationwide/International) | KES 500 |
| Posta Kenya | 3–7 days (Nationwide) | KES 100 |

**Step 3 — Payment** (see Section A4)

**Step 4 — Review & Confirm**
Check items, address and total. Click **Place Order**. Note your **Order ID** (e.g. `SY-2026-00412`).

## A4. Payments

### M-Pesa STK Push — Recommended
1. Select **M-Pesa** and enter your phone number
2. Click **Pay KES [amount]**
3. A push notification arrives on your phone — enter your **M-Pesa PIN**
4. Confirmation SMS arrives — order is auto-confirmed

> The push notification expires in **60 seconds**. If it times out, go back and retry.

### Visa / Mastercard (Stripe)
Enter your card number, expiry and CVV. You may be redirected to your bank's 3D Secure page. Approve and return.

### PayPal
You are redirected to PayPal's page. Log in and confirm. You return to SokoYetu with the order confirmed.

### PesaPal
Choose from M-Pesa, Airtel Money, card or bank on the PesaPal page and complete payment there.

### Cash on Delivery (COD)
Available on select vendors only. Pay cash to the rider on arrival. Always verify items before paying.

## A5. Tracking Your Order

Go to **My Orders** (top right → your name → Orders), find your order and click **Track**.

### Order Status Meanings
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Order placed, awaiting payment confirmation |
| **Confirmed** | Payment received, vendor notified |
| **Processing** | Vendor packing your items |
| **Shipped** | Handed to delivery partner |
| **Out for Delivery** | Rider is on the way |
| **Delivered** | Items received ✅ |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled — see reason |
| **Refunded** | Refund sent to your M-Pesa / card |

Once shipped, a **tracking code** appears. Use it on the delivery partner's website (Sendy, G4S, DHL, etc.).

You receive an **SMS and email** at each major status change.

## A6. Returns & Refunds

### When You Can Return
Within **7 days** of delivery if: item differs from description, arrived damaged, wrong item sent, or item is counterfeit.

### How to Return
1. **My Orders → Request Return**
2. Select reason and upload 1–3 photos
3. Submit

The vendor has **48 hours** to respond. If declined, admin steps in.

### Refund Timeline
| Method | Timeline |
|---|---|
| M-Pesa | 1–3 business days |
| Visa / Mastercard | 5–10 business days |
| PayPal | 3–5 business days |
| Wallet Credit | Instant |

## A7. Bulk Orders for Schools

For schools, NGOs, churches and institutions ordering large quantities at discounted prices.

### How to Bulk Order
1. Click **Bulk Order** in the nav or on any product page
2. Select your tier: 50–99 (10% off), 100–499 (20% off), 500+ (30% off)
3. Enter product/category needed and your institution name
4. Click **Request Bulk Quote** — our team calls within **24 hours**

For official invoicing, upload your **LPO** when our team follows up. We provide a VAT invoice.

## B9. Registering as a Vendor

### Requirements
- Valid National ID or Passport
- KRA PIN (individual or business)
- M-Pesa registered line for payouts

### Steps
1. Go to **sokoyetu.co.ke/vendor/register** or click **Start Selling Free**
2. Fill in shop name, county, town, description, KRA PIN, and ID number
3. Upload clear photos of your National ID (front + back) and KRA PIN certificate
4. Submit — reviewed within **24–48 hours**
5. You receive an email and SMS on approval; your shop at `sokoyetu.co.ke/vendor/[slug]` goes live

If rejected, the email states the reason. Fix and reapply — no limit on attempts.

## B11. Listing Products

**Path:** `/vendor/products/new`

### Required Fields
| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Product Name | Be specific — "Samsung Galaxy A55 128GB Black" |
| Category | Pick the most specific sub-category |
| Images | 1–8 images, min 800×800px, first image is the hero photo |
| Price (KES) | The selling price |
| Stock | How many units you have |
| Unit | piece / kg / litre / set / dozen / box |

### Optional Fields
Original price (shows a % off badge), Swahili name, bulk pricing, county/town, and description.

### Photo Guidelines
Use real photos of your actual product. Well-lit, plain background preferred. No watermarks or text overlays. No downloaded stock images.

After submitting, your product is reviewed by admin within **24 hours**, then goes live.

### Editing / Deleting Products
- **Edit:** My Products → Edit → make changes → resubmit (price/image changes go back through quick review)
- **Delete:** My Products → ⋮ → Delete. Active orders for deleted products are still fulfilled.

## B12. Fulfilling Orders

**Path:** `/vendor/orders`

You receive an in-app notification, SMS and email for each new order.

### Fulfilment Steps

**Step 1 — Confirm (within 4 hours)**
Open the order, verify items and address, click **Confirm Order**. If unable to fulfil, click **Cancel Order** with a reason.

**Step 2 — Pack**
Pack securely. Include the **Order ID** on a note inside the package.

**Step 3 — Hand to delivery & get tracking code**
Contact the delivery partner shown on the order. Get the tracking number from them.

**Step 4 — Mark as Shipped**
On the order page: **Mark as Shipped** → enter tracking code → select delivery partner → Save. Buyer is notified instantly.

**Step 5 — After delivery**
Once the buyer confirms receipt (or 7 days after shipping), earnings are credited to your balance.

## B13. Earnings & Payouts

### How It Works
1. Buyer pays (e.g. KES 1,000)
2. SokoYetu deducts 5% commission (KES 50)
3. KES 950 credited to your balance after delivery is confirmed
4. Request payout when balance ≥ KES 500

### Requesting a Payout
**Dashboard → Earnings → Withdraw** → confirm M-Pesa number → enter amount → **Request Payout**. Processed within **1–3 business days**.

### Earnings Report Columns
- **Gross Sales** — total paid before commission
- **Commission** — platform fee deducted
- **Net Earnings** — amount credited to balance
- **Pending** — from orders not yet delivered
- **Available** — ready to withdraw

## C18. Security & Privacy

### Keeping Your Account Safe
- Never share your password or M-Pesa PIN — SokoYetu staff will **never** ask for your PIN
- Use a unique password (not the same as M-Pesa PIN or email)
- Enable 2FA (see C15)
- Log out on shared devices

### What We Collect
Name, email, phone (for account and orders), delivery address, ID/KRA PIN (vendors only), order and payment history, device type and browser (for security).

### How We Protect It
All traffic over **HTTPS / TLS 1.3**. Passwords stored as **bcrypt hashes** — never plain text. Card data handled entirely by **Stripe** (PCI-DSS Level 1) — SokoYetu never stores full card numbers. M-Pesa data handled by **Safaricom Daraja API**.

### Your Rights
- Request your data: privacy@sokoyetu.co.ke
- Delete your account: Profile → Danger Zone → Delete Account
- Opt out of marketing: click Unsubscribe in any email

## C19. Frequently Asked Questions

**Is SokoYetu free for buyers?**
Yes — completely free. No fees, no subscription.

**How do I know a vendor is real?**
Approved vendors show a ✅ Verified badge — their National ID and KRA PIN have been checked by our team.

**My M-Pesa payment went through but order isn't confirmed. Help?**
Wait 5 minutes. If still pending after 10 minutes, go to My Orders → [Order] → Payment Issue. Or contact support with your M-Pesa receipt number.

**Can I change my address after ordering?**
Yes, if the order is still Pending or Confirmed. Go to the order and click Edit Address. Once Shipped, the address is locked.

**How long does delivery take?**
Nairobi: 45 minutes to 4 hours. Nationwide: 1–5 business days. International: 5–10 days via DHL.

**When do vendors get paid?**
Earnings are credited after delivery is confirmed (or 7 days after shipping). Payout to M-Pesa takes 1–3 business days after requesting.

**Can I sell second-hand items?**
Yes — list in the correct category and clearly state "Second-hand" and the condition (Good / Fair / Poor).

**What items are banned?**
Weapons, drugs, counterfeit goods, adult content, wildlife products, and anything illegal under Kenyan law.

**The app won't install on my iPhone.**
Make sure you're using **Safari** — not Chrome or Firefox. PWA installation on iPhone requires Safari.

**How do I contact a vendor?**
On any product page, click **💬 Message Vendor**. You must be logged in. Messages are monitored by SokoYetu for safety.
